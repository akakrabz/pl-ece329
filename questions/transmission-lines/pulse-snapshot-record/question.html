The generator launches $\tau_gf_i$, $\tau_g = Z_0/(R_g+Z_0)$; each end multiplies by its $\Gamma = (R-Z_0)/(R+Z_0)$. At time $t$ the first forward copy is $\tau_gf_i(t-z/v)$ and the first echo is $\tau_g\Gamma_Lf_i(t+z/v-2T)$, with $T = \ell/v$; a copy is nonzero only where its argument lies inside the pulse. $V$ adds the copies; $Z_0I$ subtracts the backward ones. On a snapshot a forward pulse lies reversed compared with $f_i(t)$ and an echo lies in source order; at a fixed point every copy arrives in source order.
