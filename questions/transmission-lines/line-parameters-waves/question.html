Every two-conductor line with a homogeneous filling has $\mathcal{C} = \epsilon\,\mathrm{GF}$ and $\mathcal{L} = \mu/\mathrm{GF}$ with one geometrical factor GF ($W/d$ for plates, $2\pi/\ln(b/a)$ for a coax). Then $v = 1/\sqrt{\mathcal{L}\mathcal{C}}$ and $Z_0 = \sqrt{\mathcal{L}/\mathcal{C}} = \eta/\mathrm{GF}$. A function of $t - z/v$ (or of $z - vt$) travels toward $+z$; of $t + z/v$ (or $z + vt$), toward $-z$. A forward wave has $I = +V/Z_0$, a backward one $I = -V/Z_0$, and the power carried along $+z$ is $VI$.
