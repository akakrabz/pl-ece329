A step source with open-circuit voltage $f_i(t) = {{params.Vg}}\,u(t)\ \mathrm{V}$ and {{{params.Rg_txt}}} is connected to the end $z = 0$ of a lossless line with characteristic impedance $Z_0 = {{params.Z0}}\ \Omega$ and length $\ell = {{params.ell_s}}\ {{{params.lu}}}$, filled with {{{params.er_txt}}}. At $z = \ell$ the line is {{{params.load_txt}}}. The line carries no voltage or current for $t \lt 0$. Use $c = 3\times10^8\ \mathrm{m/s}$.

Conventions (Lecture 28): $+z$ points from the generator toward the load. $V(z,t)$ is the voltage of the upper conductor relative to the lower one, and $I(z,t)$ is the current flowing along $+z$ in the upper conductor (returning in the lower one). Times are counted from the switch-on at $t = 0$.

(a) Find the voltage $V^+$ of the first wave launched onto the line (before any echo returns), the load reflection coefficient $\Gamma_L$ and the generator reflection coefficient $\Gamma_g$ (both for voltage).

(b) Find the voltage at {{{params.zp_desc}}}, at the three times $t_1 = {{params.t1_s}}\ {{{params.tu}}}$, $t_2 = {{params.t2_s}}\ {{{params.tu}}}$ and $t_3 = {{params.t3_s}}\ {{{params.tu}}}$.

(c) Find the current at {{{params.zq_desc}}}, at $t = {{params.tq_s}}\ {{{params.tu}}}$, positive when it flows along $+z$ in the upper conductor.

(d) Find the voltage and current on the line long after the switch-on ($t\to\infty$).

$v = c/\sqrt{\epsilon_r}$ and $T = \ell/v$. Until the first echo returns, the line looks like a resistor $Z_0$, so the generator launches $\tau_gf_i$ with $\tau_g = Z_0/(R_g+Z_0)$. Each end reflects voltage by $\Gamma = (R-Z_0)/(R+Z_0)$ with its own resistance. Forward segment $n$ passes $z$ at $z/v + 2nT$; backward segment $n$ at $2T - z/v + 2nT$. For a step, $V(z,t)$ is $V_g$ times the running sum of the weights of the segments that have crossed $z$ before $t$. For $I$, divide each weight by $Z_0$ and flip the sign of the backward ones. As $t\to\infty$ the line is just two wires: use the DC voltage divider.
