One sheet: $\mathbf{H} = \tfrac12\,\mathbf{J}_s\times\hat{n}$, with $\hat{n}$ the unit normal from the sheet toward the field point: uniform, of size $\tfrac12|J_s|$, and reversed across the sheet. A slab of thickness $w$ is a stack of thin sheets $dJ_s = J\,du'$: outside it acts like one sheet carrying $Jw$, and inside it the field changes linearly, passing through zero on the slab's centre plane.
