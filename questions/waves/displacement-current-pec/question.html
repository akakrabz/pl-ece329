{{#params.facts}}{{{text}}} {{/params.facts}}
The displacement current density is $\partial\mathbf{D}/\partial t$, with $\mathbf{D}$ (not $\epsilon_0\mathbf{E}$). Just above the bottom plate, Gauss's law gives $D_z = \rho_s$ of that plate. For $H_\phi$, apply $\oint_C\mathbf{H}\cdot d\mathbf{l} = \int_S(\mathbf{J} + \partial\mathbf{D}/\partial t)\cdot d\mathbf{S}$ to a circle in the mid-plane and the flat disk it bounds. Take the conductor as medium 2 and free space as medium 1, so $\hat n$ points out of the conductor. With $\mathbf{H} = 0$ inside, $\hat n\times(\mathbf{H}_1 - \mathbf{H}_2) = \mathbf{J}_s$ becomes $\mathbf{J}_s = \hat n\times\mathbf{H}_1$. Just outside, $\mathbf{E}$ must be normal to the surface and $\mathbf{H}$ tangential.
