Before solving anything, decide in which of the regions $x \lt 0$, $0 \lt x \lt d$ and $x \gt d$ the two fields point in opposite directions. In an outer region, also ask whether the weaker charge is the closer one. In the region you chose, set $|q_1|/r_1^2 = |q_2|/r_2^2$ and take the square root first ($|q_2|/|q_1|$ is a perfect square here). Squaring throws away directions, so the other root of the quadratic is not a null point. For (c), add the two Coulomb fields as vectors, each with $\hat{R}$ pointing from the charge to $P$.
