Each charge contributes $q_i/(4\pi\epsilon_0R_i)$ to the potential, where $R_i$ is the distance from charge $i$ to the point. Add the contributions as signed numbers (no components, no unit vectors), after converting cm to m and nC to C. In a slow move the kinetic energy does not change, so the agent's work is the change in potential energy: $W_{\text{ext}} = \Delta U = q_3\,[V(B) - V(A)]$, whatever the path. The field does the opposite amount of work, $W_{\text{field}} = -W_{\text{ext}}$.

(a) Distances in cm: $r_{1A} = {{{params.rA1_tex}}}$, $r_{2A} = {{{params.rA2_tex}}}$, $r_{1B} = {{{params.rB1_tex}}}$, $r_{2B} = {{{params.rB2_tex}}}$. Potentials add as signed numbers: $$V_A = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\left(\frac{q_1}{r_{1A}} + \frac{q_2}{r_{2A}}\right) = 8.988\times10^{9}\left(\frac{ {{params.q1}}\times10^{-9} }{ {{{params.rA1_m}}} } + \frac{ {{params.q2}}\times10^{-9} }{ {{{params.rA2_m}}} }\right) = {{{params.tA1}}} + {{{params.tA2}}} = {{{params.VA_str}}}\ \mathrm{V},$$ $$V_B = 8.988\times10^{9}\left(\frac{ {{params.q1}}\times10^{-9} }{ {{{params.rB1_m}}} } + \frac{ {{params.q2}}\times10^{-9} }{ {{{params.rB2_m}}} }\right) = {{{params.tB1}}} + {{{params.tB2}}} = {{{params.VB_str}}}\ \mathrm{V}.$$

(b) Carried slowly, $q_3$ gains no kinetic energy, so the agent's work equals the change in its potential energy: $$W_{\text{ext}} = q_3\,[V_B - V_A] = ({{params.q3}}\times10^{-9}\ \mathrm{C})\,({{{params.dV_paren}}}\ \mathrm{V}) = {{{params.W_J}}}\ \mathrm{J} = {{{params.W_str}}}\ \mu\mathrm{J}.$$ No path was needed: the static field is curl-free, so any route from $A$ to $B$ gives the same work.

(c) {{{params.c_words}}} The agent's work and the field's work cancel because the kinetic energy of $q_3$ is the same at both ends; that does not make $\Delta U$ zero.
