Isolated plates keep their charge $Q$; a connected battery keeps the voltage $V_0$. With $C(x) = \epsilon_0A/x$ for a gap $x$, write the stored energy with the right variable held fixed: $W = Q^2x/(2\epsilon_0A)$ at fixed $Q$, or $W = \epsilon_0AV_0^2/(2x)$ at fixed $V$. $W_{\text{you}} + W_{\text{battery}} = \Delta W_{\text{stored}}$, where the battery does work $V_0\,\Delta Q$ (none if it is disconnected). The force on one plate is its charge times the field of the other plate alone, $\rho_s/(2\epsilon_0)$: $F = Q^2/(2\epsilon_0A)$ at any instant.
